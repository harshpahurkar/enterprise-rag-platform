# Halvorsen Pike Advisory Employee Handbook

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Operations. Applies to all full-time and part-time staff of Halvorsen Pike Advisory.

## Welcome

Halvorsen Pike Advisory is a 140-person consulting firm with offices in Toronto and Halifax. This handbook covers the policies you will use most: time off, remote work, expenses and conduct. Questions go to your manager first, then to People Operations at people@example.com.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees earn 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. After five years of service the allowance rises to 25 days. PTO accrues monthly, so in your first year you earn it in twelve equal parts, and new hires in their first year receive a prorated amount based on start date.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried days expire on 31 March. Any days above the cap are lost without payment.

Requests of three days or fewer need one week of notice. Requests longer than three days need three weeks of notice. Your manager approves PTO in the HR portal and should answer within two business days. During the quarter-end close (the last five business days of March, June, September and December), PTO is limited to emergencies.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Each employee has 8 paid sick days per year. A medical note is required only after three consecutive sick days.

The firm closes between 24 December and 1 January. Those days do not count against PTO.

## Remote work

Halvorsen Pike runs a hybrid model. The core in-office days are Tuesday and Wednesday. Everyone is expected to be in their home office on those days unless they are at a client site. The other three days are remote by default.

Employees may work from outside their home province for up to 20 days per calendar year with manager approval. Working from another country needs written approval from People Operations and Legal at least 30 days ahead, because of tax and data residency rules. Nobody may work remotely from a country outside Canada while handling client data without that approval.

Remote staff receive a one-time home office stipend of $600 and a monthly internet allowance of $50. The stipend can be spent on a chair, desk, monitor or similar items, with receipts submitted within 60 days of purchase.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern. Be reachable on chat during those hours.

## Expenses

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Late claims need written approval from your director.

- Meals while travelling: up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
- Client dinners: up to $120 per person, with the names of attendees recorded on the claim.
- Flights: economy class for any flight under six hours. Premium economy is allowed above six hours.
- Hotels: up to $260 per night in Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal, and up to $200 per night elsewhere.
- Ground transport: use the corporate rideshare account where possible. Personal vehicle mileage is paid at $0.68 per kilometre.

Any single expense over $500 needs approval from your manager before you spend. Any single expense over $2,500 needs approval from the Finance Director. Split purchases to avoid these limits are treated as a policy violation.

The corporate card is for business spending only. Personal charges must be repaid within 14 days, and repeated personal use may lead to the card being cancelled.

## Code of conduct

We expect honest, respectful behaviour from everyone, in the office, online and at client sites.

**Respect.** Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. This includes comments, messages and jokes that target a person's race, gender, age, religion, disability or any other protected characteristic.

**Conflicts of interest.** Disclose any financial interest in a client, vendor or competitor to Legal before you take part in a decision involving that party. Outside work for a competitor is not allowed.

**Gifts.** You may accept a gift from a client or vendor only if it is worth $50 or less. Anything above $50 must be declined or handed to People Operations. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

**Reporting concerns.** Raise a concern with your manager, with People Operations, or through the anonymous Ethics Line at 555-0142. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is a dismissible offence. Investigations begin within five business days of a report.

## Performance and review

Reviews run twice a year, in June and December. Each employee sets three goals with their manager in January. Mid-year feedback is written and shared in the HR portal.

## Leaving the firm

Resigning employees give two weeks of notice, or four weeks for director level and above. Return all devices and badges on your last day. Your access to client systems ends at 17:00 on that day.
