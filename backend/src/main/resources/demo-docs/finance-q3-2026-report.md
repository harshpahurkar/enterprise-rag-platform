# Q3 2026 Financial Report

Confidential, Finance only. Prepared by Rhys Kowalczuk, Finance Director, for the Executive Committee. Period covered: 1 July to 30 September 2026. Figures are in Canadian dollars and unaudited.

## Summary

Halvorsen Pike Advisory had a strong third quarter. Revenue reached $18.4 million, up 11% on Q3 2025 and 4% above plan. Gross margin was 41.2%, compared with 39.5% in the same quarter last year. Operating profit was $3.1 million, a margin of 16.8%. Cash at quarter end was $9.6 million.

Utilisation, the share of available hours billed to clients, averaged 74%. The target is 72%, and the improvement came mostly from the Consulting practice finishing two large engagements ahead of schedule.

## Revenue by practice

| Practice | Q3 revenue | Share | Change on Q3 2025 |
|----------|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| Strategy and Operations | $7.2M | 39% | +9% |
| Technology Advisory | $6.1M | 33% | +17% |
| Risk and Compliance | $3.5M | 19% | +6% |
| Managed Services | $1.6M | 9% | +8% |

Technology Advisory grew fastest, driven by data platform work. Risk and Compliance grew slowest, because two audits moved into Q4.

## Margins and costs

Cost of delivery was $10.8 million, of which salaries and benefits were $8.9 million. Subcontractor spend fell to $0.9 million from $1.4 million a year earlier, as more work moved to employees.

Overheads were $4.5 million. Rent in Toronto and Halifax was $0.7 million, and technology and software spend was $0.8 million. Travel and entertainment came to $0.6 million, which is 8% under budget.

The average billing rate rose 3.2% to $212 per hour. Write-offs were 1.4% of billings, within the 2% limit.

## Top client accounts by billing

Billings in the quarter from the five largest clients totalled $7.9 million, or 43% of revenue.

1. Corvane Freight Ltd.: $2.6 million. Logistics network redesign and the data platform build.
2. Brightmoor Utilities Inc.: $1.9 million. Regulatory reporting programme.
3. Tallgrass Foods Co-operative: $1.4 million. Cost transformation.
4. Eastlake Credit Union: $1.1 million. Risk model validation.
5. Norvale Health Partners: $0.9 million. Operating model review.

No single client exceeded 15% of revenue. The treasury policy requires a review whenever one client passes 20%. Corvane's share is 14.1%.

Accounts receivable stood at $11.2 million, with an average collection period of 47 days against a target of 45. Brightmoor Utilities has $0.6 million outstanding beyond 60 days, and the account team is following up.

## Cash and balance sheet

Operating cash flow was $3.4 million. Capital spending was $0.3 million, for laptops and office fit-out in Halifax. The revolving credit line of $5 million remains undrawn.

## Outlook for Q4

Forecast revenue for Q4 is $19.3 million, with gross margin near 40%. The two audits that slipped from Q3 are scheduled to bill $1.2 million. Year-end bonuses are accrued at a firm factor of 1.1, based on current performance. Hiring plans add 9 consultants by 1 December.

## Risks

The main risks are client concentration, slow collections from Brightmoor, and a forecast fall in Managed Services margin from 28% to 25% as two contracts reprice in November. The Finance team also tracks a possible 1.5% increase in the Halifax lease from 1 January.
