# 2026 Compensation Framework

Confidential, HR only. Owner: Marguerite Oyelaran, Head of People Operations. Approved by the Executive Committee on 28 November 2025. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Overview

This document sets salary bands, bonus rules and review timing for 2026. Bands are annual base salary in Canadian dollars. They were benchmarked against a survey of 22 comparable advisory firms and set at the 60th percentile of that market. Nothing here may be shared with employees outside HR and the compensation committee.

## Salary bands by level

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|---------|
| L1 | Analyst | $62,000 | $70,000 | $78,000 |
| L2 | Consultant | $78,000 | $90,000 | $102,000 |
| L3 | Senior Consultant | $100,000 | $115,000 | $130,000 |
| L4 | Manager | $128,000 | $145,000 | $162,000 |
| L5 | Senior Manager | $155,000 | $175,000 | $195,000 |
| L6 | Director | $185,000 | $215,000 | $245,000 |

New hires are offered between the minimum and the midpoint. An offer above the midpoint needs written approval from the Head of People Operations. Offers above the maximum are not permitted. If a candidate expects more, consider a level change instead.

An employee paid above the band maximum is "red-circled". Red-circled staff keep their salary but receive no base increase until the band catches up.

## Annual merit increases

The 2026 merit budget is 3.5% of payroll. Managers allocate it by rating:

- Exceeds expectations: 5% to 6%
- Meets expectations: 3% to 4%
- Partially meets: 0% to 1%
- Does not meet: 0%

Increases take effect on 1 April 2026 and appear in the 15 April payroll. A promotion increase is separate and averages 8%, with a floor of 6%.

## Bonus policy

Every employee at L1 to L5 is eligible for an annual performance bonus. Directors take part in the separate partner profit pool.

Target bonus as a share of base salary:

- L1 to L2: 8%
- L3: 10%
- L4: 15%
- L5: 20%

The final payout is the target multiplied by two factors: a firm factor from 0.5 to 1.5, set by the Executive Committee after year-end results, and an individual factor from 0 to 1.5, set by the performance rating. Bonuses are paid in the first payroll after 1 March of the following year. An employee must be employed on the payment date to receive it, and those who joined mid-year are prorated by months served.

A retention bonus of $5,000 is available for hard-to-fill engineering roles. It is paid after 12 months of service and is repaid in full if the person resigns within 24 months.

## Referral awards

An employee who refers a hire that stays 6 months earns $2,500. For L4 and above the award is $4,000. HR pays the award in the next payroll after the six-month mark. Managers and HR staff involved in the hiring decision are not eligible.

## Benefits and allowances

The firm pays 80% of extended health premiums for employees and 60% for dependants. The learning budget is $1,800 per person per year. The wellness account is $500 per year.

## 2026 compensation review calendar

- 5 January: bands published
- 1 March: prior-year bonuses paid
- 15 March: merit allocations due from managers
- 1 April: increases effective
- 30 September: mid-year market check

## Pay equity

An annual pay equity review is run each May by an external reviewer. In the 2025 review, the unadjusted gap between men and women was 2.1%, and the adjusted gap was 0.6%. Any gap above 2% within a level triggers a correction in the next merit cycle.

## Exceptions

A request outside this framework goes to the Head of People Operations with a one-page business case. The Executive Committee reviews exceptions monthly. Approved exceptions are logged in the compensation register with an expiry of 12 months.
