# Master Services Agreement: Corvane Freight Ltd.

Agreement reference MSA-2025-014. Effective date: 1 March 2025. Between Halvorsen Pike Advisory (the "Advisor") and Corvane Freight Ltd. (the "Client"). This is an internal summary of the executed agreement for the Legal team. The signed original is held in the contracts vault.

## 1. Parties and scope

The Advisor provides consulting and technology services to the Client under statements of work (SOWs) issued under this agreement. Each SOW names the deliverables, fees, timeline and team. If an SOW conflicts with this agreement, this agreement prevails unless the SOW states that it overrides a specific clause.

The Client's primary contact is its General Counsel, Lucía Fenwick-Adeyemi, at lucia.fenwick@example.com. The Advisor's engagement partner is Gareth Solheim, reachable at 555-0172.

## 2. Term and renewal

The initial term is 36 months, ending on 28 February 2028. It renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the term.

## 3. Fees and payment

Fees are set in each SOW. Invoices are issued monthly and are due within 45 days of receipt. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1% per month. The Client may dispute an invoice in good faith within 15 days of receipt, and must pay any undisputed part on time.

Rates are fixed for the first 24 months. After that, the Advisor may raise rates once per year, by no more than 4% and on 60 days of written notice.

## 4. Limitation of liability

Each party's total liability under this agreement is capped at the fees paid or payable to the Advisor in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits.

The cap does not apply to the following:

- breach of the confidentiality obligations in section 5;
- the Advisor's indemnity for third-party intellectual property claims;
- fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct;
- the Client's obligation to pay fees.

A separate super-cap applies to data breach claims. The Advisor's liability for a breach of the data protection terms is capped at two times the annual fees, which is higher than the general cap.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other's confidential information with at least the care it gives its own, and never less than reasonable care. Confidential information may be used only to perform or receive the services. It may be shared with employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know and who are bound by equivalent duties.

The obligations last for the term and for 5 years afterwards. Trade secrets stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets. The duty does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was already known to it, or was independently developed.

A party compelled by law to disclose must give prompt notice, where lawful, and disclose only what is required.

## 6. Data protection and retention

The Advisor processes the Client's personal data only on the Client's documented instructions. Personal data is stored in Canada. The Advisor must report any confirmed breach affecting Client data within 48 hours, which is shorter than the 72 hours in the Advisor's own internal policy. The 48-hour deadline controls for this Client.

Client data is retained for the term of each SOW and then deleted or returned within 60 days of its end. Backups are purged within a further 30 days. The Client may request an earlier deletion on 10 business days of notice. Records that the Advisor must keep by law are retained for 7 years and are kept separate and unused for other purposes.

The Client may audit the Advisor's data protection controls once per year on 30 days of notice, during business hours. Audit costs are borne by the Client unless the audit finds a material breach.

## 7. Intellectual property

Deliverables created specifically for the Client belong to the Client once paid for in full. The Advisor keeps its pre-existing tools, methods and templates, and grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use them as part of the deliverables.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement for convenience on 90 days of written notice. Either party may terminate for cause if the other commits a material breach and fails to fix it within 30 days of written notice. Either party may terminate immediately on the other's insolvency.

On termination, the Client pays for all work done up to the termination date and approved expenses. If the Client terminates for convenience before 28 February 2027, it also pays an early exit fee of $150,000. After that date no exit fee is due.

Sections on confidentiality, liability, intellectual property and data retention survive termination.

## 9. Governing law and disputes

The agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario. Disputes go first to the executive sponsors for 20 days of good-faith talks, then to mediation, and finally to binding arbitration in Toronto before a single arbitrator.

## 10. Notices

Notices are in writing and are sent to the General Counsel of the Client and to the Advisor's Legal department at legal@example.com. Email notice counts as received on the next business day.
