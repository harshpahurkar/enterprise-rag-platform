# Halvorsen Pike Advisory Information Security Policy

Version 4.2, approved 15 February 2026. Owner: Security Office, led by Priya Nandakumar, Chief Information Security Officer. Applies to all employees, contractors and interns.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how we classify, store, share and report on information. Our clients trust us with sensitive data, and a single mistake can end a client relationship. Read this document during onboarding and again each January.

## Data classification

All information falls into one of four classes.

1. **Public.** Material approved for release, such as marketing pages and published thought leadership.
2. **Internal.** Everyday business information that is not meant for outsiders, such as meeting notes and project plans.
3. **Confidential.** Client deliverables, contracts, financial results and personnel files. Access is limited to people who need it for their work.
4. **Restricted.** Data that would cause serious harm if exposed: personal identifiers, payment card data, credentials, and anything a client has marked as regulated.

If you are unsure, treat the data as one level higher than your first guess. Documents are labelled in the footer or file name. Unlabelled documents default to Internal.

## Client data handling

Client data stays inside approved systems. That means the client's own environment, our encrypted project workspace, or the secure file share. It never goes to personal email, personal cloud storage or consumer chat apps.

Work on client data happens on firm-managed laptops only. Personal devices may read email and calendar but may not hold client files. Laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Client data is stored in Canadian regions only, in the Toronto and Montreal data centres. Moving it elsewhere needs written approval from the CISO and the client.

When an engagement ends, project data is returned or destroyed within 60 days, unless a contract sets a different period. The project lead confirms destruction in writing and logs it in the records register.

Copies of client data must not be pasted into public AI tools. Use only the firm-approved assistant, which masks personal data before text leaves our network.

## Personal information rules

Personal information (PII) includes names combined with email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, Social Insurance Numbers (SIN), employee numbers and payment card numbers.

- Collect only what the engagement requires. If a spreadsheet has a SIN column you do not need, delete the column.
- SIN and card numbers are Restricted. They may be stored only in the HR system or the payments vault, never in documents, tickets or chat.
- Mask personal data in screenshots, slides and examples. Use obviously fake values in demos and training.
- Do not send PII by plain email. Use the secure share and set a 14-day expiry on the link.
- Access to Restricted data is reviewed every quarter. Managers confirm or remove each person's access.

## Passwords and access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Use the firm password manager and never reuse a password. Multi-factor authentication is required for every system, and hardware security keys are required for administrators.

Access follows least privilege. When you change teams, your old access is removed within 5 business days. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## Incident reporting

A security incident is any suspected loss, theft, exposure or misuse of firm or client data. Examples include a lost laptop, a phishing link you clicked, a misdirected email with client files, or a colleague sharing a password.

Report it at once. Do not wait to confirm it is real.

- Email security@example.com or call the 24-hour hotline at 555-0177.
- You must report within 1 hour of noticing. Late reporting is a policy breach, but good-faith reports are never punished.
- Do not delete anything, and do not try to investigate on your own.

The Security Office triages each report within 2 hours and assigns a severity. For any incident involving client personal data, Legal decides on notification to the client and regulators. Where notification is required, the firm tells affected clients within 72 hours of confirming the breach.

After closure, the Security Office writes a blameless review within 10 business days and shares lessons with the wider firm.

## Phishing and social engineering

Phishing simulations run every month. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phish button in the mail client. Staff who fail three simulations in a rolling 12 months complete a 30-minute refresher course.

Never act on a request to change bank details or send gift cards without a call-back to a known number. Finance will never ask you to move money by chat.

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions to this policy need written approval from the CISO and expire after 90 days unless renewed. Violations may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination. Wilful exposure of Restricted data may also be referred to the authorities.
